[Kiwix](https://kiwix.org) is an organization that helps make web content available for offline viewing. I've been working with them since 2018 and I would say that I'm definitely part of the organization, in a volunteer capacity. I primarily work with them on [[wp-1.0|WP1]], though I've also worked on [mwoffliner](https://github.com/openzim/mwoffliner), which is a scraping bot for making offline copies of Wikipedia pages. The offliner works with WP1 inside something called a [Zimfarm](https://github.com/openzim/zimfarm).
