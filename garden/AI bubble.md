In April, I wondered if there was an AI bubble in the US stock market/economy.

At this point **it is abundantly clear that there is**. In fact, we might be already past the point where investors and banks are trying to deflate the bubble gently, for a _soft landing_ of sorts. Apparently Doctorow predicted this [back in January of 2026](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/ng-interactive/2026/jan/18/tech-ai-bubble-burst-reverse-centaur). And [Ed Zitron](https://www.wheresyoured.at/) lays out a compelling case with specific economic numbers.
