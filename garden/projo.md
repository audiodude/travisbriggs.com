[Projo](https://projo.toolforge.org) is a tool for helping Wikipedia editors, who are coordinating editathons or other group editing projects, find good lists of articles that they can work on. There are some particular criteria for such a list:

1. The articles should have some content already
2. They should have some relative amount of "importance", as measured by pageviews
3. They should be marked as needing work or needing cleaning up
4. They probably shouldn't be BLP (Biographies of Living Persons), since those require special care.

You start with a list of articles, or a category (thanks [Petscan](https://petscan.wmcloud.org/)!), or anything that the [[mw-selections|Global Selection Picker]] can select, and the tool gathers data based on these criteria. From there you are presented with a list of articles that you can download in .tsv format.
