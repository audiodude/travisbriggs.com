Things I've made, maintained, contributed to, and tried. Some are useful tools, some are creative experiments, and some never quite became what I imagined.

This is a map into the garden, not a list of currently maintained services. Some of the linked pages haven't been planted yet.

## Wikipedia and Wikidata

- [[wp-1.0|WP1]] - Wikipedia article assessments, selections, and offline collections. I rewrote the bot and portal in 2018 and have been maintaining it since.
- [[projo|Projo]] - Finding useful Wikipedia editing and review work, as well as New Pages Patrol tools.
- [[findlinkfast]] - Finding unlinked mentions and reviewing proposed Wikipedia links. It's a rewrite of Edward Betts's _find link_.
- [[linkrecnext]] - Link recommendations from other language editions. My fork of Martin Gerlach's tool.
- [[citewright|CiteWright]] - Turning Wikipedia citation templates into Wikidata references. A Wikipedia "user script".
- [[langpow]] - Faster language selection for monolingual-text values on Wikidata. Another "user script".
- [[linkdata]] - Reusable datasets about Wikipedia's link graph.
- [[mw-selections|Global Selection Picker]] - A portable format and picker for lists of Wikimedia pages. Used by [[projo]], [[findlinkfast]] and [[linkrecnext]]. And hopefully soon to be used by [[wp-1.0|WP1]]
- [[wiki-event-ics-button|Wikipedia event calendar script]] - Adding Wikipedia events to a personal calendar. Another "user script".
- [[wiki-pride-article-suggestions|Pride article suggestions]] - Preparing article worklists about LGBTQ publications and media. This was the original inspiration that became [[projo]].

## Instruments and audio experiments

- [[lambda-sequencer|Lambda Sequencer]] - A modular browser MIDI sequencer with patch cables and portable patches.
- [[arpsplit]] - A Max for Live device that distributes incoming notes across MIDI channels.
- [[remapperator|Remapperator]] - A Max for Live MIDI-remapping utility.
- [[web-lpd8|Web LPD8]] - A browser-based editor for an Akai LPD8 MIDI controller.
- [[vape-station|Vape Station]] - A JUCE synthesizer prototype.
- [[infinite-radio|Infinite Radio]] - Experiments with phrase-aligned jumps, indefinite playback, and audio mashups.
- [[neural-noise|Neural Noise]] - Earlier machine-learning experiments generating music from [ABC notation](https://abcnotation.com/). Pre-dates LLMs, using a technique called a chaRNN (Character Recurrent Neural Network).
